今年，我五十歲了。

This year, I turned fifty.

這個數字來得比想像中安靜。沒有什麼特別的轉折，日子照常：上班、開會、帶團隊、解決一個又一個技術問題。只是某天停下來，忽然意識到，人生大概已經走了一半。

The number arrived more quietly than I expected. No dramatic turning point. Life went on as usual: work, meetings, leading a team, solving one technical problem after another. Then one day I paused and realized I am probably about halfway through.

## 為什麼開始寫 | Why write now

當了多年工程師，我習慣把事情量化、記錄、分析。實驗有紀錄，設計有版本，失敗有檢討。可是對於自己的生活和想法，我卻很少留下任何紀錄。

After many years as an engineer, I am used to measuring, recording, and analyzing. Experiments have logs, designs have versions, failures get post-mortems. Yet for my own life and thoughts, I have kept almost no record at all.

所以我想開始寫。不是為了給別人看，而是想給十年後的自己一份紀錄：五十歲的我在想什麼、在意什麼、懷疑什麼。

So I want to start writing. Not mainly for others, but as a record for myself ten years from now: what I was thinking about at fifty, what I cared about, what I doubted.

> 五十歲不是終點，而是重新看見的起點。
>
> Fifty is not an ending. It is a place to start seeing again.

## 這裡會寫什麼 | What you'll find here

這裡會有三類文章：生活裡的小事、對工作與世界的想法，以及對過去的省思。每篇都會用中英文並排書寫，一方面練習，一方面也希望讓不同語言的朋友都能讀到。

There will be three kinds of posts: small moments from daily life, thoughts on work and the world, and reflections on the past. Every post is written in Chinese and English side by side, partly as practice, and partly so friends in either language can read along.

不求寫得多，只求寫得真。

I don't aim to write a lot, only to write honestly.
