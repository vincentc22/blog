1. **自利偏誤**：每個人都容易覺得自己有權做想做的事，例如花超過收入的錢。蒙格舉莫札特為例：他是世上最有名的作曲家，大多時候卻過得很痛苦，原因之一就是總是入不敷出。
2. **四種災難性的思考方式**：一般來說，嫉妒、怨恨、報復和自憐，都是災難性的思考方式。
3. **別陷入自憐**：自憐很接近偏執，而偏執是最難扭轉的事情之一。一直可憐自己，久了就會覺得全世界都虧欠你。

<!-- -->

1. **Self-serving bias:** We all tend to feel entitled to do what we want, like spending more than we earn. Munger points to Mozart, the world's most famous composer, who was miserable much of the time, partly because he always overspent his income.
2. **Four disastrous modes of thought:** "Generally speaking, envy, resentment, revenge, and self-pity are disastrous modes of thought."
3. **Don't drift into self-pity:** Self-pity comes close to paranoia, one of the hardest things to reverse. Pity yourself long enough, and you start to feel the whole world owes you.
